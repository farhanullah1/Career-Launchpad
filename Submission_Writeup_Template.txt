WEEK 5 — TASK 05
POWER BI — DATA IMPORT, TRANSFORMATION & INTERACTIVE DASHBOARD

Dashboard Title:
Financial Performance & Sales Analytics

Dataset:
Microsoft Power BI Financial Sample.

Objective:
The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales, profit, cost of goods sold, units sold, market segment, country/region, product, and time-based performance. The report is designed to move from raw financial data to a decision-ready visual summary.

KPIs:
1. Total Sales — overall sales value.
2. Total Profit — total profit generated.
3. Profit Margin % — profit as a percentage of sales.
4. Total Units Sold — total quantity sold.
5. Sales YoY % — year-over-year sales change where the date range supports it.

Visuals:
• KPI cards at the top.
• Line chart for sales/profit trend over time.
• Clustered bar chart for sales by country.
• Donut chart for sales by segment.
• Bar chart for top products.
• Matrix/table for country, sales, profit, and margin.
• Slicers for Date, Segment, Country, and Product.

Key insights to report:
• Identify the highest-sales country/region from the dashboard.
• Identify the highest-profit product or segment.
• Identify the strongest/weakest period in the time trend.
• Comment on the relationship between sales and profit.

Note:
The exact values and rankings should be taken from the final Power BI dashboard after the dataset is loaded and filtered. Do not write invented numerical findings.
